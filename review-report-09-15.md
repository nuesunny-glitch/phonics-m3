## Real issues — Chapter 13 (Error Detection)

1. **Exercise item 10 is a malformed sentence with two conflicting time markers:**
   > "10. They (A)went (B)to the beach (C)next week (D)last year."

   This glues together "next week" (future) and "last year" (past) in one sentence, which doesn't read as coherent English regardless of which part is "wrong." The answer key entry is also confusing as written:
   > "10 | D | next week (ไม่ใช่ last week) หรือแก้ next→last ให้สอดคล้อง | เลือกอย่างใดอย่างหนึ่งให้ Tense กับคำสัญญาณตรงกัน — went ต้องคู่กับ last week ไม่ใช่ next week"

   It hedges between two different fixes and even mismatches "last week" vs. the sentence's actual "last year." → *Recommended fix:* rewrite as a single clean sentence with one time marker, e.g. "They (A)went (B)to the beach (C)last week (D)with their family," with a clean single-answer key entry.

2. **Quiz item 6 breaks the chapter's established error-detection pattern:**
   > "6. My birthday (A)is (B)in (C)May (D)on every year." → answer: "ง) D — ตัดคำว่า "on" ออก (is in May every year)"

   Every other Error Detection item in this chapter (and in Ch15's Part E, which draws on the same 8 error points) is a **word-swap** type ("this word is wrong, replace with that word"). This item instead requires **deleting** an extra word entirely — a different task type that's never explained in the chapter's instructions or the "8 จุดที่ควรตรวจสอบ" checklist. Students trained on the word-swap pattern could reasonably get this "right" by picking the wrong replacement instead of realizing the fix is deletion. → *Recommended fix:* either rewrite so the fix is a normal word-swap (e.g., change "on" to a wrong-but-swappable form), or explicitly add "extra/missing word" as a 9th checklist item so it's not sprung on students unannounced.
